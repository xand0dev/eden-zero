You don’t control life. You define its laws.

People wake beside fresh water with 341-neuron spiking brains, a genome and no instructions. Every step they take — eating, building, farming, digging canals — is the output of their brain. Write the laws of their world, intervene sparingly, and try to understand what comes of it.

The one rule

Nothing in this world is scripted

There is no findFood(), no behaviour tree and no state machine. Innate reflexes exist only as ordinary synapses that a lifetime of learning is free to rewrite. What you watch is what the brains, the bodies and the ecology produce — and you can open any brain and see the spikes that just fired.

What you do

Write the laws. Watch. Understand.

You are an observer with a budget, not a ruler. Favour grows when the world thrives; you spend it on rain, food, a blessing — or a pulse of reward into one brain.

Open any brain

Real spikes along real synapses, the reward signal it learns from, and the synapses a life has rewritten — gold where they grew, violet where they faded.

Seasons and eras

An eight-day year: spring growth, summer thirst, autumn harvest, winter snow. Hearth, camp, village, town, city — read from the world, never unlocked by a button.

Fate, with a day’s warning

Drought, harsh winter, predator migration, blight, flood, fever, wildfire, eclipse. Crises change the physics of the world; how a village copes is up to its brains — and to what you do with the warning.

An atlas of the unwritten

An observational classifier names what it sees — the Provider, the Night Watch, the Irrigator, the Mourner — with a stated criterion you can check. Epithets follow people into the chronicle and their obituaries.

Found on the way

The brains were erasing their own reflexes

Thirsty people stood at the shore and did not drink. The measurement: the innate thirst → drink synapse fell from 1.80 to 0.05 in ten simulated minutes, in every brain — and turning its plasticity off did not stop it.

The cause was homeostatic synaptic scaling: it held each neuron’s total input constant, so as learning grew a motor neuron’s other inputs, the reflex was scaled away with them. Scaling now leaves the reflex arcs alone; over thirty thousand ticks they hold at 1.78.

“Measure the derived quantity.” Every fix in this project started with a probe that measured what the brain was actually doing — the thirst probe, the drink-weight probe, the steering probe — and the failures stay in the implementation log.

How it is built

A pure, deterministic core

Simulation

  • Strict TypeScript, no ML library
  • Leaky integrate-and-fire network, reward-modulated Hebbian learning from the body’s own physiology
  • Same seed + same commands = byte-identical history; a replay is a few hundred bytes

Observatory

  • React + PixiJS diorama, the world in a Web Worker
  • Canvas brain view with real spikes and a porthole into the world
  • Saves packed as exact float32, gzip in IndexedDB

Evidence

  • 202 unit and integration tests
  • Headless acceptance and multi-seed balance harnesses
  • Probes for every claim, in scripts/
